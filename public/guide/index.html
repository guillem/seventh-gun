Guide

Everything you need to survive a run: controls, the seven guns, how the maze is laid out, what the difficulty settings actually change, and how seeds and shared maps work.

There is no jump, no crouch and no sprint. Movement is one speed, 6.5 units per second, so positioning is about corners and doorways rather than mobility tricks.

Phone and tablet

A floating stick appears under your left thumb to move. Drag on the right side of the screen to look. FIRE, USE, MAP and pause buttons sit above the HUD on the right.

A run, start to finish

  1. You spawn with the Viper Pistol, 70 bullets and 100 HP.
  2. The maze is a spine of 12–14 rooms from your start to the antechamber, with 3–4 loot spurs hanging off it and up to two alternate links. Corridors are three cells wide; a cell is two world units.
  3. Guns 2 to 6 sit along the route in strictly increasing order, so a detour always pays something better than what you already carry.
  4. Two or three doors stand between you and the end. At most one is locked, and the key only ever guards a vault spur — the arena is never key-gated.
  5. Gun 7, the Seventh, waits in the antechamber. Picking it up shatters the seal on the finale arena.
  6. Clear the arena to win. Its enemies are placed when the maze is generated, so there is no way to sneak past the fight — the room is the last thing between you and the end.

On Normal a generated maze holds 55–75 enemies across an 88×88 cell grid, with two or three open courtyards under the sky dome and the rest under a 4.2-unit ceiling. A full run takes roughly 20 to 30 minutes.

Seeds

A seed is a short string. The same seed on the same skill level produces identical geometry, enemies and loot, on any machine — for as long as the generator version stays the same. The version is stamped into the API as generatorVersion, and a generator change deliberately rebuilds every maze, so a seed saved today still plays, but a generator upgrade is a new map.

  • Type a seed in the SEED field, or press RND for a random one.
  • https://seventhgun.com/?seed=nightjar presets the field, so you can link a specific run.
  • After a run, COPY LINK gives you the shareable URL, and MAP LOG on the title screen lists every seed you have played with its time, skill and outcome. The log lives in this browser only.
  • The layout is generated before difficulty is applied, so the same seed is the same maze on Easy, Normal and Hard — only enemies, damage and loot change. Racing a friend across difficulties is a fair comparison of skill, not of map luck.

The campaign

Seven authored maps, played in order. The guns you collect stay with you between maps; your health resets at the start of each one. Map 1 is always open, and each win unlocks the next. Progress is saved in this browser, so CONTINUE picks up where you left off.

Arena

Small-team deathmatch on a generated 96×96 grid, up to ten players, no monsters and no jump. You spawn with the pistol and 70 bullets; gun, ammo and medikit pads are spread across the map. Spawn protection lasts two seconds or until your first shot. Respawn is 25 seconds, 40 for the Seventh, 12 for ammo and 20 for medikits. TAB shows the scoreboard, M the map, and the fight continues while a menu is open — you stay vulnerable. Idle players are dropped after two minutes.

Map editor

The editor lets you draw a map by hand, set its title and seed, test it, and export it as a compact SGMAP.v1. code. A code pasted into https://seventhgun.com/#m=<code> boots straight into that map, which is how custom maps are shared.

Practical advice

  • Take every spur. The gun order is monotonic, so a detour can only improve your loadout.
  • Doors are chokepoints. Back into one and the chaingun's spread stops mattering.
  • The Bile Launcher and the Seventh both hurt you. In a corridor, the splash is your enemy as much as theirs.
  • The vault is optional loot, never a route. If the fight is going badly, skip it.
  • Nothing in the arena is key-gated. If you are lost, head for the sky.
