FAQ

Short answers. Longer ones live in the guide.

Is Seventh Gun free?

Yes. Free to play, no ads, no in-game currency, no paid tier. Nothing to buy and no account to create.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in the browser at seventhgun.com and loads about 250 kB over the wire, so there is no download, launcher or asset pack.

Does it work on a phone or tablet?

Yes. On touch devices a floating stick appears under your left thumb to move, dragging on the right side looks around, and FIRE, USE, MAP and pause buttons sit above the HUD.

How long does a run take?

Roughly 20 to 30 minutes for a generated maze on Normal. The campaign is seven maps played in sequence, so it is longer overall.

How many guns are there?

Seven: Viper Pistol, Ripjaw Shotgun, Hornet Chaingun, Spiker, Bile Launcher, Sunlance and the Seventh. You always start with the pistol and find the rest during the run. The about page lists them with damage and fire rate.

Is there multiplayer?

Yes, an arena mode for up to ten players on a generated 96×96 map, with no monsters and no jump. It needs the game's optional arena server, which runs on Cloudflare Workers or a self-hosted Node process.

Can I play offline?

Not from seventhgun.com. The source is MIT-licensed and can be built and served locally, which works offline once loaded, but the hosted game needs a network connection to fetch its bundle.

How do seeds work?

A seed is a short string that determines the entire maze: geometry, enemies and loot. The same seed and skill level always produce the identical run on any machine, until the generator version changes. That makes a seed a shareable challenge. The layout is generated before difficulty is applied, so the same seed is the same maze on every skill level.

Can I share a specific run with someone?

Yes. https://seventhgun.com/?seed=nightjar presets the seed, and COPY LINK after a run gives you that URL. The MAP LOG on the title screen remembers every seed you have played.

Can I make my own maps?

Yes. The built-in editor lets you draw a map, title it, test it and export it as a compact SGMAP.v1 code, which plays when pasted after #m= in the game URL.

Does it save my progress?

Progress, settings and the map log are stored in your browser's local storage. They survive reloads, but not clearing site data, a different browser or a private window.

What do I need to play it?

A browser with WebGL, on any desktop or mobile operating system. Keyboard and mouse, or touch. There is no gamepad support.

Is it open source?

Yes, MIT licensed. The full source, including the optional arena server, is on GitHub.

What is art.seventhgun.com?

The same game with an experimental art treatment instead of the classic look. It is a separate build deployed from its own branch; gameplay, seeds and maps are identical.

Why does it look like a 1997 game?

That is the intent. Seventh Gun is deliberately built in the Wolfenstein and Doom lineage: chunky textures, a fixed 1/60 second simulation step, and guns that are distinct in feel rather than in spreadsheets.

Something is broken. Where do I report it?

Open an issue at github.com/guillem/seventh-gun/issues. Include your browser, whether you were on desktop or touch, and the seed if the run itself misbehaved.
