# Third-party software

Every texture, mesh, sound and level in SEVENTH GUN is generated at runtime from
a seed. There are no asset packs, no fonts, no CDN scripts and no third-party
art of any kind, so the MIT license in [LICENSE](LICENSE) covers the whole work.

One third-party library is bundled into the shipped client:

## three.js

- License: MIT
- Copyright 2010-2026 Three.js Authors
- https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js

Its `@license` banner is preserved verbatim inside the built bundle
(`dist/client/assets/*.js`), so distributing a build satisfies the MIT
attribution requirement with no further action.
